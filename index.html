How Financial Lab works.

4.0.9.1 APP WALKTHROUGHPowered by Dexx

Financial Lab helps you decide what to do with each paycheck before the money gets spent. Cover what matters, prepare for what is coming, save on purpose, pay down debt intelligently, and know what is truly safe to spend.

YOUR FRIDAY ROUTINE5 steps
1
Enter your paycheck

Open Budget Lab and enter your check amount, check date, and next payday.

2
Review your bills

Dexx checks what is due before the next payday and looks farther ahead for bills that need money protected now.

3
Read Dexx’s recommendation

Dexx separates Pay Now, Bills Reserve, Savings, Extra Debt, and TRUE Safe-to-Spend.

4
Approve the payday plan

Approval is when Reserve Memory and Savings Goals actually record the money you chose to protect.

5
Spend from TRUE Safe-to-Spend

This is what remains after protected bills, future reserves, savings, and planned debt money are accounted for.

THE LABSWhat each section does

🧪 Laboratory

Your home dashboard: Financial Health Score, missions, progress, savings, debt, and Dexx observations.

💵 Budget Lab

The payday engine. Enter a check, review bills, see reserves, savings and debt recommendations, then approve the plan.

💳 Credit Lab

Add each debt, track balances and minimums, and choose Balanced, Snowball, or Avalanche targeting.

💰 Savings Lab

Create multiple savings goals and let Dexx prioritize by importance, deadline, or quickest goal to finish.

IMPORTANT CONCEPTSKnow these numbers
Pay Now

Bills that need to be funded before your next payday.

Bills Reserve

Money Dexx recommends protecting from this check for bills coming later. It is not spending money.

Reserve Memory

After you approve a plan, Dexx remembers what is already protected for each future bill so the next paycheck starts from the remaining amount, not zero.

Savings Target

The goal Dexx recommends for this paycheck’s savings amount based on your Savings Lab strategy.

Debt Target

The account Dexx recommends for extra debt money based on Balanced, Snowball, or Avalanche strategy.

TRUE Safe-to-Spend

The number to watch. It is what remains after immediate bills, future obligations, savings, and planned debt money are protected.

WHY APPROVAL MATTERSRecommendation ≠ action

You can rebuild a payday plan as many times as you want while testing numbers. Dexx does not treat recommendations as real until you tap Approve This Payday Plan. Approval writes Reserve Memory and Savings Goal contributions into your saved financial data.
